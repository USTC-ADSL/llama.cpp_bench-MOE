An amateur orchestra is preparing a concert in a hall it has never used before. Rehearsal time is limited, several players commute from distant towns, and the stage layout affects how well the musicians can hear one another. The conductor wants a rehearsal plan that addresses difficult passages without exhausting the ensemble. Explain how to collect feedback, choose priorities, and check whether a problem comes from timing, balance, or the acoustics of the room. Discuss contingency plans for a missing player and for a shortened sound check. Include a way to communicate last minute changes clearly, and describe how the orchestra can learn from the concert without blaming individuals for every mistake.
