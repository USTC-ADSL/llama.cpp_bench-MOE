A cooperative of vegetable farmers wants to reduce water use during a dry summer. Their fields have different soils, some wells contain more salt than others, and crop prices change from week to week. A single irrigation schedule would be easy to manage but may waste water or damage sensitive plants. Describe a decision process that combines basic field observations with inexpensive measurements. Explain how to choose a small experiment before changing the schedule across all farms. Discuss how to divide maintenance responsibilities, what to record after unexpected rain, and how to avoid favoring the largest landowners when allocating scarce water. Include a plan for reviewing the results at the end of the season and for communicating mistakes honestly.
