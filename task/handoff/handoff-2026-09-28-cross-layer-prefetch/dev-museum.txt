A small museum is cataloging photographs of a coastal town. Many images have no dates, and several buildings have changed names. Volunteers disagree about whether two photographs show the same market square. Some photographs are damaged and cannot safely be handled every day. The curator wants a catalog that distinguishes documented facts from uncertain guesses. Design a practical workflow for inspecting the collection, recording uncertainty, and checking claims against maps and newspapers. Explain how to preserve the original objects while making useful digital copies available to researchers. Discuss what to do when a donor asks to correct a description without providing evidence. Include a way to record conflicting interpretations without discarding either the image or the provenance of earlier descriptions.
