A teacher is preparing a science project for a class whose students have very different reading abilities. The project compares how plants grow under different light conditions. There are not enough measuring instruments for every student, and the classroom is available only during school hours. Design an experiment that lets each group contribute useful observations while keeping the comparisons fair. Explain how to assign responsibilities, record measurement uncertainty, and handle plants that die before the end of the project. Include a method for helping students distinguish an observation from an explanation. Discuss how the teacher can assess careful reasoning without rewarding only the group with the tallest plant, and how to share results with parents.
