A team is planning a scientific survey of a cave system with narrow passages and occasional flooding. Existing maps were drawn by different groups and use inconsistent labels. Researchers want to document water flow and small animal populations while minimizing disturbance. Propose a sequence of preparations, measurements, and safety checks for a short expedition. Explain how to decide which areas can be visited during uncertain weather, how to label observations so another team can reproduce them, and how to handle equipment that stops working underground. Include rules for preserving fragile formations and for separating confirmed observations from estimates. Describe how the team should review its records before publishing a map.
