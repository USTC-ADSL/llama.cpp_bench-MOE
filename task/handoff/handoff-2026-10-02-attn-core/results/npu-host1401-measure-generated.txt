### Mobile Computing Overview

A mobile phone is a complex system that needs to perform various tasks efficiently and effectively. It contains three main types of processors: a central processor (CPU), a graphics processor (GPU), and a neural processor (NPU). Each of these processors is specialized for different types of tasks, and they all contribute to the overall performance of the device.

### Central Processor (CPU)

- **Function**: The CPU is responsible for executing instructions and managing the overall operations of the phone. It handles tasks like running applications, managing multitasking, and handling system-level tasks.
- **Latency and Energy Costs**: The CPU has a relatively high latency and consumes a significant amount of power. It is the primary processor for most tasks, but it is not optimized for high-performance tasks like graphics rendering or deep learning.
