An engineer is preparing a plan for a small reservoir that supplies drinking water to several villages. During the wet season the river brings sediment and floating branches. During the dry season demand rises while the inflow falls. Explain how to combine measurements of water level, rainfall, and daily consumption into a practical operating plan. Discuss the tradeoffs between flood protection and storing enough water for a long drought. Include a realistic schedule for inspecting the intake, recording observations, and communicating changes to the village council. Avoid assuming that every sensor reading is correct or that tomorrow's weather forecast is certain.
